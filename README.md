## About the Game:

A game about an unlucky farmer out to discover the truth behind their family's curse. After your father's untimely passing, he leaves you his farm. But, he has outstanding debt he left behind. It's up to you to clear the debt by the end of the week to save your father's legacy. Be careful though, you inherited your father's bad luck; so it may be a challenge. 

  <br>



## Downloading the Game:

Go to the releases page and select your OS, then download the corresponding zip file containing the binary. We support both Windows & Linux platforms currently, MacOS could theoretically run the Linux binary but that is untested at this time.

  <br>



## Minimum Requirements:

  - x86/x64 CPU
  - 4 GB of RAM
  - Integrated Graphics
  - 2.5 GB of Storage
